  • Tax codes vary by individual and may include adjustments for benefits, underpayments, or other factors

  • Pension contribution rates and thresholds may be revised
  • National Insurance thresholds may change in budget announcements
  • Student loan thresholds are updated annually and may differ from those used in the Calculator
  • The Calculator does not account for overtime, unsocial hours enhancements, or other additional payments
